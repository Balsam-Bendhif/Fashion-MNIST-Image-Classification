Model Card¶

Dataset¶

Fashion-MNIST Dataset

  • 70,000 grayscale images
  • Resolution: 28 × 28 pixels
  • 10 clothing categories

Intended Use¶

This model is intended to classify clothing images into one of ten predefined fashion categories.

Performance¶

Baseline Model Accuracy: 84.34%

CNN Accuracy: 91.28%

Limitations¶

The dataset contains low-resolution grayscale images and does not represent real-world fashion photographs.

Bias and Risks¶

The model may not generalize to colored images, different camera angles, or unseen fashion styles.

Failure Cases¶

The model struggles to distinguish between visually similar categories such as:

  • Shirt
  • T-shirt/top
  • Pullover
  • Coat

These classes share similar visual characteristics.
